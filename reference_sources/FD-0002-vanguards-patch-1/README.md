## Colonisation economy (quoted sentences)

- "the goods produced by the top two economies will no longer be consumed by ports or settlements linked to that economy"
- "This is intended to resolve instances where economies would 'cannibalize' each other via market links"

The patch notes do not say how the top two are ranked, or what happens to their
demand. The Mega Guide's community research answers both (MG-0001, PDF page 69):
rank by strength (tie-breaker presumed alphabetical, footnote 26), and supply is
protected while demand is not.
