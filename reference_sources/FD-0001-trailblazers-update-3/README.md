## Colony-type port economy overrides (quoted items)

Body type, then the economies it overrides:

- "Black holes, Neutron Stars, White Dwarves": "HighTech", "Tourism"
- "Brown Dwarves and all other star types": "Military"
- "Earth like worlds": "Agriculture", "Hightech", "Military", "Tourism"
- "Water world": "Agriculture", "Tourism"
- "Ammonia world": "HighTech", "Tourism"
- "Gas giant": "HighTech", "Industrial"
- "High metal content and metal rich world": "Extraction"
- "Rocky ice": "Industrial", "Refinery"
- "Rocky": "Refinery"
- "Icy": "Industrial"
- "Has rings (includes stars with asteroid belts)": "Extraction"
- "Has organics": "Agriculture", "Terraforming"
- "Has geologicals": "Extraction", "Industrial"

Quoted sentence: "These overrides may stack."

## Links (quoted sentences)

- "Strong links are created between a port and any facility located on or around the same local planetary body."
- "Weak links are created between ports and supporting facilities located on different bodies within the same system"
- "A strong link provides a higher economic boost than a weak link"
- "Strong links are subject to boosts or decreases in economic supply performance by the characteristics of the host system or body."
- "Weak links are unaffected by this mechanic."

The post gives no numeric link strengths.

## Strong link boosts and decreases (itemised, not verbatim)

- Agriculture. Boosted: orbiting an Earth-like world; on or orbiting a
  terraformable body; on or orbiting a body with organics. Decreased: on or
  orbiting an icy body; on or orbiting a planet tidally locked to its star;
  orbiting a moon tidally locked to its planet, where that planet is also
  tidally locked to the star.
- Extraction. Boosted: a system with major or pristine resources; on or
  orbiting a body with volcanism. Decreased: a system with low or depleted
  resources.
- High Tech. Boosted: orbiting an ammonia world; orbiting an Earth-like world;
  on or orbiting a body with geologicals; on or orbiting a body with organics.
  Decreased: nil.
- Industrial and Refinery. Boosted: a system with major or pristine resources.
  Decreased: a system with low or depleted resources.
- Tourism. Boosted: orbiting an ammonia world; a system with a black hole;
  orbiting an Earth-like world; on or orbiting a body with geologicals; on or
  orbiting a body with organics; orbiting a water world; a system with a white
  dwarf; a system with a neutron star. Decreased: nil.

Water World appears among the boosts **only for Tourism**. The Mega Guide also
lists "Orbiting a Water World" as an Agriculture and a High Tech boost (MG-0001,
PDF pages 55-56); see contradiction `C-0012`.

## Population (quoted sentences)

- "Populations will grow on each weekly maintenance tick."
- "Populations growth is based on a curve and will grow quickly for the first month before slowing to a more gradual pace,"
- "Populations within colonised systems will now grow at a significantly faster rate"
- "Overall population capacity remains determined by the port and facility types built within a system."

The Mega Guide's measured data says the first growth ticks are daily (MG-0001,
PDF page 77, footnote 27); see contradiction `C-0010`.
