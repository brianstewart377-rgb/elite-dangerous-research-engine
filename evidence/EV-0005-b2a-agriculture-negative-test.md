# EV-0005 - B2a Agriculture Negative Test

## Evidence type

Live build test / negative economy result.

## Source

- Source name: Brian live report in ChatGPT conversation
- Captured by: Brian
- System: Wregoe colony project
- Body: B2a
- Evidence phase: after live construction and market inspection
- Review status: user-confirmed observation; no screenshots attached in this record

## Tested build

Brian built the following on B2a:

- 1 x Ceres Large Agriculture Settlement
- 1 x Space Farm

Both were subsequently demolished after the market result was judged poor.

## Direct observations

- The resulting Agriculture market contained roughly 8 commodities.
- The observed commodity selection was not useful for the intended carrier-provisioning / colonisation-material goal.
- Desired Agriculture-side goods such as Fruit and Vegetables and Food Cartridges were not observed.
- The Agriculture Settlement's approximate 100% Agriculture score was unaffected by the Space Farm.
- The Space Farm's approximate 100% Agriculture score was unaffected by the Agriculture Settlement.
- The Space Farm did not appear to deepen or strengthen the Ceres market in the way expected.

## Interpretation

The test suggests that on B2a, facility-only Agriculture without a natural or body-level Agriculture bonus created only a shallow Agriculture market.

Top-two economy protection is therefore not sufficient by itself to guarantee a useful commodity range. Agriculture may be protected as an economy while still being too weak or too basic to expose the desired goods.

## Additional hypothesis from Brian

A Civilian Port might have benefited more directly from a Space Farm link, but would likely have inherited the body's stronger natural economy as well. On B2a this may have resulted in something like Extraction plus weak Agriculture, rather than a strong Agriculture supply market.

This is a hypothesis only. It has not been tested in the current evidence record.

## Conclusion

Reject B2a as a useful Agriculture / carrier-provisioning body for Wregoe unless future live preview evidence shows a stronger Agriculture score or a different routing mechanic.

Do not assume that adding a Space Farm to a neutral body will boost a nearby ground Agriculture market. Treat Space Farm and Ceres-style Agriculture Settlement stacking as unproven unless a live preview shows the target market's score increasing.

## Confidence

- Directness: High for the reported live outcome.
- Specificity: High for B2a Ceres + Space Farm test.
- Corroboration: Medium-low because screenshots are not attached to this committed record.
- Overall confidence: High that this specific B2a Agriculture plan failed; medium for the inferred cause.

## Planning impact

- Do not rebuild the same B2a Ceres + Space Farm Agriculture plan.
- Do not spend additional B2a slots trying to force Agriculture without a body bonus.
- Prioritise Wregoe's proven useful families: Extraction, Refinery, Industrial, and High Tech.
- Source Agriculture-side materials from a companion system or a body with a genuine Agriculture bias if needed.
